How this book came to be

Some places keep calling to us long after a journey has ended. For me, Istanbul and Tangier are such places. As I visited and photographed them, I felt a bond that went beyond resemblance: two different cities, both overlooking a passage of water, both accustomed to seeing people, languages and worlds arrive and depart.

This book was born from that feeling. Istanbul on the Bosphorus and Tangier by the Strait of Gibraltar become the Sisters of the Straits. I searched their histories for the reasons behind that connection and imagined listening to what their stones might have told us.

Here, the cities themselves speak. In the first person, they guide us from their foundations through the modern age to the present, recalling the names they have borne, the peoples who have inhabited them and the transformations that have changed their faces. They remember glories and wounds, preserve legends and recognize themselves in the traces left by each era.

My diaries capture journeys I have lived. The Books, by contrast, grow from the themes those journeys awaken: curiosity, research and the desire to give shape to a story. In this first book, photographs, historical evidence and artistic reconstructions accompany the imagined voices of Istanbul and Tangier.

We will listen to them separately and then together, as their memories touch upon the sea and one sister opens the doors to her own treasure. The journey begins with their voices.

Lorella
